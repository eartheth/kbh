Knightsbridge House

We Provide Quality International Education for Everyone

Knightsbridge House (KBH), one of the fastest growing international schools from Singapore, established its first school campus in Nonthaburi, Thailand in Aug 2025. We deliver the UK Cambridge Curriculum, with the addition of Chinese, Thai, and the Singapore approach to teaching Math and Science.

Students grow in a fun and inspiring learning environment that fosters academic excellence while developing critical thinking, creativity and character. At KBH, every child can succeed.
